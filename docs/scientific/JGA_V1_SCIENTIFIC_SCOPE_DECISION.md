# JGA v1.0 Scientific Scope Decision

Status: **LOCKED — PRINCIPAL-INVESTIGATOR APPROVED**

Decision date: 2026-09-02

Decision authority: Scientific Advisor decision following the accepted
Bass-recovery evidence synthesis at commit
`782bd89e313d2fb2c90a5f8f54c9922e341c8f0d`

## Decision

JGA v1.0 is focused on the quantitative observation of timing relationships
within the jazz rhythm section for the purpose of reconstructing the historical
evolution of jazz timing.

The primary v1.0 analytical source scope is:

- Drums;
- Double Bass; and
- Piano.

Voice / Lead Vocal and lead or solo instruments are outside the scientific
scope of v1.0.

## Interpretation

This is a project-scope decision, not an instrument ontology, automatic source
classifier or provenance-bound role assignment. It does not change the
Observation, Translation, Core or Domain boundaries and does not modify
`RhythmSectionTimingProfile` behavior.

Inclusion in the primary scope does not imply equal scientific maturity,
observability or validation status. In particular, the frozen Bass-recovery
synthesis establishes that mixed-audio Double Bass observability remains
source-attribution-limited and authorizes no new recovery mechanism or
production behavior. Existing Piano and Drum evidence likewise retains its
own dataset-, authority- and claim-specific limitations.

The v1.0 exclusion of Voice and lead/solo instruments supersedes earlier
project-state language that described Voice as merely deferred, but only for
the bounded v1.0 scientific scope. It does not make a permanent claim about a
future JGA version or erase historical Voice/lead validation records.

## Scientific and architectural consequences

- Existing scientific results and historical evidence remain unchanged.
- Existing production behavior remains unchanged.
- No source receives analytical authority solely from its instrument name.
- No physical onset, musical correspondence, source identity or equal
  validation status is implied.
- Work outside this scope requires a future explicit scientific-scope decision;
  it is not part of v1.0 completion.

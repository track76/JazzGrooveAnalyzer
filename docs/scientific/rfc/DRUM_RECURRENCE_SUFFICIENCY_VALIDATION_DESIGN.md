# Drum recurrence-sufficiency validation design

Identifier: DRUM-RECURRENCE-SUFFICIENCY-VALIDATION-DESIGN-01. Date: 2026-09-10.
Status: **PROPOSED DESIGN FOR PI REVIEW; NOT PREREGISTERED; NOT EXECUTION-READY.**

## 1. Decision and mission boundary

The accepted recurrence package is reusable. A conditional finite-support null comparison is designable, but its JGA-specific conditioning authority is incomplete. A complete, decisive recurrence-sufficiency criterion cannot yet be preregistered without inventing authority. Recommend resolving the null's observation support and nuisance conditioning first, then preregistering a bounded evidence-characterization experiment. Do not promote a small conditional p-value into general recurrence sufficiency.

Scientific Question: Does the frozen population exhibit recurrence organization not accounted for by a justified observation-domain nuisance model, with explicitly bounded temporal coverage and robustness?

Direct Contribution to JGA Mission: distinguish empirically supported temporal organization from all-pairs and measurement-induced structure before later musical interpretation.

Missing Scientific Evidence: a warranted conditional comparison, dependence-aware uncertainty, and a justified rule for interpreting its relevance to the next stage.

Why Existing Evidence Is Insufficient: exact witness recovery establishes existence and numerical reproducibility, not a stochastic baseline, physical robustness or persistence.

Smallest Experiment: one whole-population conditional randomization comparison, with one primary recurrence statistic and separate temporal-support diagnostics; no audio or periodicity response computation.

Architectural Impact: None. Complexity Introduced: an experiment-local null specification and independently checked evaluation, only after further authorization; no production representation or implementation now.

The Research Constitution requires: “When a scientifically simpler path exists that produces equivalent evidence, the simpler path shall always be preferred.” This design does not reopen accepted map execution or Double-Bass work.

Evidence labels: OBSERVED FACT refers to inspected, authenticated records; LOGICAL INFERENCE to consequences of their contract; PROPOSED DESIGN to recommendations requiring PI approval; UNKNOWN to absent authority. The current PI instruction accepts the witness result. The historical report's pending-PI-review wording and the older bootstrap describe earlier stages, not conflicting new results. They remain unchanged.

## 3. Question, non-claims and exposure disclosure

Proposed bounded question: conditional on a justified observation lattice/producer map, fixed source and scope, and specified nuisance structure, is the whole-population recurrence statistic unusually large under the stated conditional law? Separately, where are its supporting observations located and how does support change under explicitly authorized sensitivity operations?

“Non-accidental” can mean incompatibility with a named null, never proof that chance or all measurement artifacts are excluded. An exact result is not exact physical timing. The broad requested sufficiency claim includes more than rejecting one null.

No inference of BeatReference, preferred duration, rank, tactus, meter, BPM, component identity, groove or swing. No new musical Ground Truth. The 368 relations are already admissible **neutral research candidates**; this task concerns stronger evidential support for advancement, not retrospectively revoking that admission.

This is a design after disclosure of the real counts and ranges, not a blind confirmatory analysis plan. Inspection of the governing validation protocol also exposed its embedded historical musical-reference section incidentally. No reference values are reproduced or used to choose a timescale, statistic, null or criterion. Independent method review should receive the observation contract and proposed method without musical labels; it must assess design selection explicitly. Future surrogates on this same population would not constitute a held-out performance.

## 7. Selected smallest conditional design — not yet bound

**Selected for independent review:** uniform sampling from a finite set of allowed event configurations conditional on fixed observation-time region counts and independently warranted detector constraints. This is a proposed JGA adaptation of conditional resampling, not a conclusion established by the cited neuroscience studies.

Let G be the documented set of possible observation coordinates within the unchanged scope; let f(g) be the exact forward producer binary64 encoding, decoded afterward to a rational. Let B be a predeclared partition of that scope, and n_b its observed count per region. Let A be the set of all 63-coordinate configurations satisfying these counts, uniqueness, valid endpoints and any independently documented exclusion constraints. Conditional null H0: configurations in A are equiprobable, conditional on those nuisance quantities. If uniformity is not justified, stop rather than silently substituting an estimated law.

For independent bin occupancy with no cross-bin constraints this means uniform subsets without replacement, not independent draws that allow impossible duplicate observations. If constraints cross region boundaries, the law is uniform on the **joint constrained set**; a convenient sequential sampler is not automatically uniform. The original configuration must belong to A. No hypothetical coordinate is represented as an actually measured EME. Use separate surrogate IDs and parent-of-simulation provenance; never mutate original event identities.

PRESERVES: total events, source condition, exact scope, local counts at B's resolution, permitted support, forward timestamp encoding and bound detector restrictions. All-pairs enumeration preserves the combinatorial dependence mechanism.

BREAKS: detailed placement and relations not fixed by those constraints, including exact recurrences when chance configurations do not reproduce them. It need not destroy every recurrence; that is essential. It does not preserve unconditioned interval distribution, every form of local clustering, or unknown detector correlations. Whether those are nuisance or signal requires authority before use.

Unbound essentials: G and f for unobserved coordinates; partition scale and origin; detector restrictions; adequacy of equiprobability; whether short-range patterns must be conditioned upon. The exact binary64 roundtrip in accepted data is not sufficient to fill these fields. A diagnostic producer sample coordinate must not replace accepted exact timestamps. Do not infer a physical jitter bound from frame spacing.

A small predeclared collection of justified partitions may be necessary for nuisance sensitivity. Do not scan arbitrary partitions and choose the smallest p-value. If no scientifically justified partition exists, no decisive conditional test is ready. Conditioning every observed timestamp would produce a degenerate null; allowing unconstrained uniform timing would answer an excessively broad question. Neither closes sufficiency.

No trial null, surrogate, p-value or new coverage curve was computed in this task.

## 8. Minimal statistics and selection control

Primary scalar, using the already documented endpoint type:

W(X) = sum over all positive exact relations T of n_X(T) * 1[n_X(T) >= 2].

This is the number of distinct unordered witnesses participating in recurrent exact relations, not an independent sample size and not a relation score. It tests an excess of repeated support; it cannot detect every possible form of temporal organization. Total pairs are fixed by count, so a second normalized statistic adds no information. The number of recurrent relations alone is not selected as primary: consolidation of many pairs into fewer exact classes can decrease that count despite stronger repeated support.

For every surrogate, enumerate its complete hypothetical observation population and apply the same exact recurrence functional to **all** its relations, including singleton classes. Do not constrain null relation values to the 368 observed members, which were selected on this very population. Original candidate membership remains unchanged. Surrogate enumeration is new prospective recurrence computation, not periodicity response recomputation, and is not authorized now.

Mandatory descriptive companion: for each exact relation preserve ordered witness intervals, unique endpoint locations, gaps, and overlap/dependence labels. At population level preserve the unique recurrent-endpoint counting function C(u) = number of recurrent endpoints at or before u, together with the corresponding all-event counting function N(u), over the full exact scope. Do not count an endpoint repeatedly because it belongs to many relations. These are support records, not another uncalibrated significance test. They cannot alone establish persistence of any one relation.

W is the only proposed primary stochastic comparison. Original witness-count distributions, endpoint participation and ratios are retained as inherited descriptive evidence. No omnibus timbre/beat/recurrence score. Adding a inferential coverage, family or change statistic requires separate justification and multiplicity control; do not silently promote diagnostics after viewing results.

## 9. Dependence and temporal coverage

The stochastic unit is the **entire conditional event configuration**. Pair counts, shared endpoints, cross-relation endpoint reuse and overlapping regions are regenerated jointly, never modeled as 1,953 independent trials. Replay supplies no extra sample. No binomial standard error using witness count, naive independent-pair bootstrap, or center-level t-test.

An optional, prospectively fixed sensitivity can remove each original event in turn and recompute the descriptive W and support references on the retained original pairs. Preserve all 63 deletions, not a chosen robust subset; no independence or confidence interval follows. This is missing-observation sensitivity, not a chance null, and is deferred unless PI includes it. Similarly, event-disjoint witness information is retained without asserting physical or statistical independence. Removing shared-endpoint witnesses from admission would change the original hypothesis and is forbidden.

Temporal categories require scale and opportunity definitions. The preserved interval/gap records support description of local concentration, separated occurrences and changing support. One long witness interval does not fill its interior with repeated events. A union of all candidate supports does not show persistence of a particular relation.

For a future scale-specific coverage test, use independently justified observation-time regions and enumerate per-relation support contained in each region versus crossing its boundaries; use whole null configurations for calibration. Empty regions, edge opportunities and count differences must survive. No bars or beat-aligned segments. Until span, gap, change and error criteria are justified, return the exact support record with category **UNRESOLVED**, rather than labeling it persistent or continuously recurrent. Do not impose a coverage percentage from 62/63 endpoint participation.

## 10. Exactness, robustness and ratio families

The accepted catalogue always uses exact rational differences reconstructed from producer bits. No perturbation is an amendment to that equality. Continuous nondegenerate independent jitter almost surely removes equalities among distinct generic positive intervals; surviving floating-point collisions then depend on the representation. That mathematical observation is not a rejection of the accepted result and is not a useful automatic sufficiency criterion.

Distinguish three operations: algebraic exact translation (integrity); conditional relocation under H0 (null comparison); perturbation under a calibrated measurement/missingness model (robustness). The third is a separate prospective experiment. Track witness IDs and retain/loss of original exact relations, plus new relations in a separate namespace. Do not merge close values. A claim about approximate structural stability requires its own justified correspondence representation; it is not currently defined. No physical uncertainty law is supplied by the present package.

All 67,528 ratios form the complete pairwise ratio graph of 368 rational values by construction. Connectivity of that graph cannot reveal nontrivial families. Exact integer-ratio subgraphs are also influenced by grid arithmetic and all-pairs identities. Low-denominator cutoffs or privileged base values would introduce unsupported selection. Preserve all ratios and lineage, but exclude connectivity, nested-scale or family scores from the minimal primary test. A later independently justified constrained relation graph could be evaluated against the same complete null construction; no graph was built or family inferred here. Multiple alternatives remain unranked.

## 11. Prospective execution and evaluation contract

This is a conditional blueprint, not execution permission:

1. Independently review and freeze G, f, A/B, nuisance assumptions, scope of inference, statistic, error policy and any sensitivity set. Verify their justification without consulting a musical reference. Record that observed W and other summaries were already known when designing this test.
2. Freeze input authority using candidate/root/replay plus the complete events, witnesses and singleton catalogue. The candidate file alone is insufficient. Recheck hashes; reject any alteration. No accepted map response or audio input is required.
3. Freeze a reviewed null sampler, independent checker, environment, seed/PRNG specification, planned simulation count and deterministic output contract. A fresh process replay uses the same declared random stream for reproducibility; it is not additional Monte Carlo evidence. Independently scheduled draws within that stream define the null simulation.
4. Before real-input use, synthetic tests must check membership/uniform sampling on exhaustively enumerable small configuration sets, impossible duplicates, boundaries, forward encoding, all-pairs accounting, exact equality, ties and cases compatible/incompatible with the chosen null. Do not tune on real null outputs. No such implementation or tests are created now.
5. In later authorized execution, generate the entire fixed set of null configurations; preserve their coordinates/encoding, constraints, seeds and statistics. No stopping on significance. Independently reconstruct statistics and verify sampler authority. An independent implementation/checker must not merely count the generator's success labels.
6. Report observed W, complete null distribution, tie-inclusive upper-tail evidence and effect relative to that conditional distribution. Under the reviewed iid conditional simulation model, a conservative Monte Carlo test uses (1 + number of surrogate W >= observed W)/(M+1). Exact validity depends on exchangeability; the formula is not a cure for a wrong null. Preserve Monte Carlo uncertainty and the distinct exhaustive conditional tail if enumerated. Never report a zero probability from zero exceedances [M4].
7. Choose M prospectively from independently approved tail resolution / simulation-uncertainty needs and resources; choose any error budget from an explicit decision policy, not a convenient convention. Neither is specified numerically here. If several nuisance models are retained, report every result; no minimum-p selection. Combining them requires a justified joint or conservative composite rule and retained uncertainty.
8. Freeze outputs, independent checker, replay and interpretation. Preserve incomplete/mismatched cases without retuning or rerunning. No per-relation rejection or admission is implied by a population-level test.

A correctly conditioned null-compatible result can contradict the proposed excess-support hypothesis. Null rejection would contradict only H0, and could indicate omitted nuisance structure. W plus descriptive coverage is a **bounded evidence-characterization experiment**, not a complete test of the broad sufficiency claim until the remaining gates are justified.

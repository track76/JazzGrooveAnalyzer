### CH04 practical borrowing specification

**PREFERRED: analog small-diaphragm cardioid condenser**, end-address pencil format, P48-compatible balanced XLR output. Externally polarized and electret condensers are both acceptable; polarization method alone is not a fidelity criterion. A small housing facilitates unobstructed repeatable positioning. Cardioid directivity gives a practicable forward excitation view with less rear pickup than an omni; it does not isolate a string or eliminate nearby body radiation. A smooth wideband response and low noise matter more than the label “studio” or “measurement.” Manufacturer guidance describes small-diaphragm condensers as useful for transient capture and precise placement; it does not validate a particular mic’s impulse response or establish scientific equivalence between categories. [RØDE technical explanation](https://rode.com/en-gr/about/news-info/the-difference-between-large-diaphragm-and-small-diaphragm-condenser-microphones).

| Characteristic | Practical request to lender | Scientific qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Frequency response | Published nominal **20 Hz–20 kHz** bandwidth and available response curve; no fixed low-cut; switchable HPF OFF | Inspect actual low-end response around 41/55 Hz and high-frequency peaks. Nominal bandwidth is not a ±dB flatness guarantee; modest documented coloration is acceptable and retained. No inverse filtering. |
| Transient behavior | Broadband instrument condenser with smooth response, no intentional resonant voicing or onboard transient processing | No scientifically supported numerical rise-time/ringing cutoff exists here. Retain published impulse/phase data if available; diaphragm size alone cannot certify this. |
| Self-noise | **≤20 dBA equivalent input noise**, preferably **≤15 dBA** | Proposed borrowing ceiling/preference, not an experimentally established minimum for O01–O08. A-weighted noise can hide low-frequency noise; retain unweighted/noise spectrum if supplied. No guarantee that late decay exceeds the actual room/chain floor. |
| Manufacturer SNR | At least **74 dB(A)**, preferably **79 dB(A)**, when referenced to 1 Pa / 94 dB SPL | Equivalent to the above noise convention, not actual Bass recording SNR. Do not compare specifications with different reference/weighting. |
| Headroom | Published maximum input **≥120 dB SPL at ≤1% THD**, with pad state declared | Conservative borrowing target for close ordinary pizzicato, not measured session SPL or proof of linearity. Keep session gain/headroom policy; record any pad choice before sealing. |
| Interface and accessories | Analog balanced XLR, compatible P48, ordinary microphone clip/shockmount, stable boom stand and cable | No USB-only/digital microphone, independent ADC, wireless link or processing stage. |

These numerical borrowing criteria are **engineering choices**, not new scientific resolvability thresholds. There is no justified universal minimum SNR that guarantees this pilot’s late partials/noise observations. A microphone outside these criteria is not thereby scientifically invalid, but needs a specific documented suitability review rather than silent substitution.

|---|---|---|
| PREFERRED | Small-diaphragm cardioid condenser meeting the table | Compact aimed excitation view with practical wideband/noise/headroom specifications. |
| ACCEPTABLE | Large-diaphragm condenser in cardioid mode meeting the same table | Can preserve the required waveform; capsule acoustic center/front must be documented, housing must not obstruct hand/video. No claim it is inherently too slow. |
| ACCEPTABLE, conditional | Low-noise small-diaphragm omni or calibrated measurement condenser meeting the table | Can capture wideband transients, but takes more surrounding acoustic field and cannot achieve the same directional emphasis. Requires documented choice of different spatial weighting; calibration certificate not mandatory. |
| NOT RECOMMENDED for this borrowing request | Typical moving-coil stage/vocal or kick microphone; typical passive ribbon; shotgun/interference-tube microphone | Often less convenient for the requested combination of wide bandwidth, close-view geometry, gain/noise or neutral response. ## 5. Physical-action authority and calibration/reference plan

Continuous iPhone video is admissible **in principle** for take, string, fingering, articulation and visible contact/damping intervals. It cannot establish hidden contact, force, subframe timing or acoustic component truth. Non-audio authority is the visible physical action, not an onset picked from the phone soundtrack.

**Proposed video mode/view:** prefer ordinary 1080p/60-fps video, stable tripod/support, native camera soundtrack, fixed camera/lens, adequate steady illumination, focus/exposure locked on hands/string plane where supported; Auto FPS off. Use landscape orientation and a fixed support; focus/exposure lock where available prevents distracting shifts but does not prove fixed exposure. Phone audio is synchronization support only. Place the camera obliquely on the unobstructed plucking-hand side with both hands, four strings, damping location and anonymous take slate visible. No per-take slate is required inside an uninterrupted episode: each take is identified by the sealed timeline and visible guide. One slate for the entire session is insufficient for checking intervening drift/restarts. Every restart gets new episode ID and its own bracketing markers. Record distances from clap location to mic capsules and camera, and preserve original timestamps/project origins. A clap is not a calibrated impulse; uncertainty from closure visibility, propagation and camera timing is retained. No specialized clock hardware is required for the bounded video-interval role.

Later independent evaluation may pair **visible clapper closure intervals** with UMC impact intervals to estimate offset/drift and retain marker residuals, propagation, exposure, rolling-shutter, visibility and interpolation uncertainty. Markers around each block allow drift checks; they do not guarantee linear drift between them. The phone soundtrack is an auxiliary matching aid, never zero-latency truth. No resampling/alignment or marker extraction is performed now. Do not reuse the note onset as its own synchronization authority. A slate supports frame-scale action evidence, not calibrated microsecond contact or inter-channel phase. If an eventual scoring tolerance is finer than supported video intervals, B03 remains unresolved; no such threshold is invented. Thus video acquires the required modality when capture-day visibility checks pass; it does not close the protected B03 annotation/scoring blocker. CUE TIME != PHYSICAL CONTACT TIME.

## Purpose

Evaluate every candidate scientific coordinate against the validation criteria
defined in G-024.

No candidate becomes part of the scientific geometric framework until all
validation criteria are satisfied.

---

# Evaluation Procedure

Each candidate shall be evaluated according to:

- Observable
- Domain Origin
- Deterministic
- Reproducible
- Traceable
- Independent

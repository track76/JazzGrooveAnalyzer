# G-017 — Metric Space Properties

Status: DRAFT

Layer: Representation

Depends on:

- G-016 Scientific Coordinate Definition

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the mathematical properties required for the
scientific coordinate space adopted by JGA.

The purpose is not to choose a mathematical space.

The purpose is to establish the properties that every future scientific
space shall satisfy.

---

# 2. Observation First

The mathematical space shall emerge from observable musical behaviour.

No mathematical structure may be imposed before scientific validation.

---

# 3. Representation Fidelity

The coordinate space shall preserve observable facts without alteration.

Projection shall never distort scientific information.

---

# 4. Determinism

Equal observable states shall always produce equal geometric states.

---

# 5. Reproducibility

Independent executions shall generate identical geometric
representations.

---

# 6. Stability

Small observational variations shall never generate arbitrary geometric
instability.

The notion of stability shall itself be scientifically defined.

---

# 7. Identity

Two geometric representations may be considered identical only if they
encode the same observable scientific information.

Graphical coincidence is insufficient.

---

# 8. Comparability

The space shall allow rigorous comparison between different
representations.

Comparison shall depend exclusively on scientific properties.

---

# 9. Extensibility

Future dimensions may be introduced only if they represent newly
validated observable properties.

Existing dimensions shall never change meaning.

---

# 10. Undefined Properties

This document intentionally leaves undefined:

- topology;
- dimensionality;
- metric;
- norm;
- neighbourhood;
- continuity;
- distance.

These properties require independent scientific justification.

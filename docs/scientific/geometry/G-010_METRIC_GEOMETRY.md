# G-010 — Metric Geometry

---

## Purpose

This document formally defines the geometric
structure of the Representation Layer.

It does not define visualization.

It defines the scientific geometry that
visualization will represent.

---

## Fundamental Principle

Metric geometry is generated from validated
musical observations.

It is never inferred.

---

## Geometric Objects

The Representation Layer is composed of the
following scientific objects.

MetricPoint

MetricTrajectory

MetricClusterPortrait

MetricLandscape

---

## Local Geometry

MetricClusterPortrait describes the local
geometry of one MetricCluster.

Local geometry never extends outside its
MetricCluster.

---

## Global Geometry

MetricTrajectory describes the temporal
evolution of the complete performance.

It connects consecutive MetricPoints.

---

## Global Representation

MetricLandscape aggregates all local and
global geometric objects.

It represents one complete performance.

---

## Geometric Traceability

Every geometric object preserves a direct
traceability to Domain objects.

No geometric object may exist without
scientific provenance.

---

## Scientific Independence

Geometry belongs to the Representation Layer.

Visualization consumes geometry.

Visualization never defines geometry.

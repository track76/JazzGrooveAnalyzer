# G-021 — Scientific Geometric Space

## Status

Draft

---

## Purpose

Define the scientific geometric framework used by the Jazz Groove Analyzer.

This document establishes the principles governing every future geometric
representation produced by the system.

No mathematical coordinate may be introduced unless it represents an
observable musical fact.

---

# Principle 1

Geometry is not visualization.

Geometry is the scientific organization of observable musical facts.

---

# Principle 2

Every geometric coordinate must derive from exactly one observable quantity.

A coordinate never represents an arbitrary mathematical convenience.

---

# Principle 3

Coordinates must be mutually independent.

Different axes describe different observable phenomena.

No coordinate may duplicate information already encoded by another axis.

---

# Principle 4

Every coordinate must be traceable.

For every coordinate it shall always be possible to reconstruct:

Observable
→ Domain Object
→ Scientific Quantity
→ Geometric Coordinate

---

# Principle 5

Geometry never modifies the Domain.

The Domain remains the scientific source of truth.

Geometry only projects observable quantities into a scientific space.

---

# Principle 6

A geometric space is deterministic.

The same musical observation always generates the same geometric projection.

---

# Initial Coordinate

The first validated scientific coordinate is:

Metric Offset

defined in G-020.

Future coordinates shall follow the same scientific methodology.

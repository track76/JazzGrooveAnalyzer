# G-024 — Scientific Coordinate Validation

## Status

Draft

---

## Purpose

Define the validation process required before introducing a new scientific
coordinate into the Jazz Groove Analyzer geometric framework.

---

# Validation Criteria

Every candidate coordinate shall satisfy all the following conditions.

## Observable

The quantity must represent a directly observable musical fact.

---

## Domain Origin

The quantity must be derivable exclusively from existing Domain objects.

No external mathematical construction is allowed.

---

## Deterministic

The same musical observation shall always generate the same coordinate.

---

## Reproducible

Independent executions shall produce identical results.

---

## Traceable

The complete chain shall always be reconstructable.

Observable
    ↓
Domain Object
    ↓
Scientific Quantity
    ↓
Geometric Coordinate

---

## Independent

The coordinate shall describe a phenomenon not already represented by another
validated coordinate.

---

# Rejection Rule

Failure of any validation criterion immediately excludes the candidate
coordinate from the scientific geometric framework.

# G-018 — Scientific Distance

Status: DRAFT

Layer: Representation

Depends on:

- G-016 Scientific Coordinate Definition
- G-017 Metric Space Properties

---

# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the scientific meaning of distance inside the
JGA geometric representation.

Distance is introduced exclusively as a measure of similarity between
observable musical behaviours.

Distance is never a graphical concept.

---

# 2. Fundamental Principle

Scientific distance shall compare observations.

It shall never compare drawings.

The geometric representation exists only because observable behaviours
can be compared.

---

# 3. Behavioural Meaning

A small scientific distance indicates that two observable behaviours are
scientifically similar.

A large scientific distance indicates that the observable behaviours are
scientifically different.

Distance therefore expresses behavioural similarity.

---

# 4. Independence

Scientific distance shall remain independent from:

- screen resolution;
- rendering;
- coordinate scaling;
- graphical transformations;
- visualization technology.

---

# 5. Scientific Constraints

Every future distance definition shall satisfy:

- symmetry;
- reproducibility;
- deterministic evaluation;
- preservation of provenance.

---

# 6. Forbidden Interpretations

Distance shall never be interpreted as:

- musical quality;
- artistic value;
- expressive quality;
- aesthetic judgement.

It measures similarity only.

---

# 7. Future Mathematical Definition

The numerical formulation of distance is intentionally deferred.

The mathematical expression shall emerge from validated observable
properties and not from predefined geometric conventions.

---

# 8. Architectural Consequence

Historical comparison,
ensemble comparison,
stylistic comparison,
and temporal evolution
shall all depend on the same scientific notion of distance.

No specialized distance definitions shall exist.

---

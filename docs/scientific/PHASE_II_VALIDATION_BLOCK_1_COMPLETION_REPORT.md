Status: COMPLETED

Date: 2026-08-09

## 1. Scope of Validation Block 1

Phase II Validation Block 1 examined whether preserved VAL-001 temporal
observations support reproducible Candidate Period evidence before musical
metric interpretation.

The block comprised the scientific foundations, controlled experiments,
immutable evidence representation, responsibility correction and production
discovery required to preserve that evidence. It did not identify beat, tempo,
meter, tactus, subdivision or metric level.

## 2. Scientific Objectives

The objectives were to:

- establish the scientific distinction between observation-derived
  periodicity and metric-level interpretation;
- define the minimum scientific meaning of Candidate Period and Candidate
  Population;
- determine whether VAL-001 contains reproducible evidence for multiple
  Candidate Periods;
- determine the minimum evidence and input population required for initial
  Candidate Period discovery;
- preserve Candidate Period evidence immutably without experimental-record
  responsibility; and
- discover the complete supported population without selection, ranking or
  musical metric interpretation.

## 5. Scientific Evidence Produced

The completed records establish the following Observed Facts within VAL-001:

- multiple exact recurrent consecutive temporal intervals coexist;
- the populations are deterministically reproducible under the preserved
  inputs and configurations;
- supporting observations, temporal scope and source provenance are
  recoverable;
- the arithmetic mean of event spacing is not itself one of the exact
  recurrent Candidate Periods in the audited populations;
- non-consecutive and cross-source recurrence provide additional descriptive
  evidence but are not indispensable to initial Candidate Period discovery;
- phase evidence was not established as an indispensable evidence dimension;
  and
- temporal distribution is recoverable from preserved occurrence evidence.

Post-blind Ground Truth comparison preserved numerical correspondence without
assigning beat, tempo, subdivision, hierarchy or metric equivalence.

## 6. Canonical Scientific Conclusions

- Candidate Periods are pre-interpretive, observation-derived temporal
  relations supported by reproducible recurrence evidence.
- Multiple Candidate Periods may coexist and must be preserved without
  premature reduction to one period.
- Metric-level interpretation is distinct from Candidate Period discovery.
- Ground Truth evaluates already-discovered evidence and does not participate
  in blind discovery.
- Exact recurrence, supporting observations, provenance and declared scope
  are sufficient for the first production discovery responsibility within the
  evidence tested by VAL-001.
- No additional representation or evidence dimension was scientifically
  demonstrated as indispensable within this validation block.

These conclusions are limited to the preserved repository evidence and do not
establish musical metric meaning for any Candidate Period.

# F-012 — Behaviour Scan

Status

Draft

------------------------------------------------------------

Purpose

Behaviour Scan is the primary scientific observation process
performed by Jazz Groove Analyzer.

Its objective is not to compute a distance.

Its objective is to identify behavioural evolution.

------------------------------------------------------------

Scientific Principle

JGA observes Behaviour.

Only afterwards does it quantify Behaviour.

------------------------------------------------------------

Observation Process

Scientific Behaviour Space

↓

Continuous Behaviour Scan

↓

Behaviour Change Events

↓

Scientific Report

↓

Derived Behaviour Distance

------------------------------------------------------------

Observable Behaviour

Behaviour Scan identifies

• stable regions

• progressive divergence

• sudden divergence

• convergence

• recovery

• oscillation

------------------------------------------------------------

Output

Behaviour Scan produces a chronological sequence of
Behaviour Change Events.

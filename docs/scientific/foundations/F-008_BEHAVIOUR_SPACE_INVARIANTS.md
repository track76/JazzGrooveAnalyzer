# F-008 — Behaviour Space Invariants

## Status

Draft

---

# Purpose

This document defines the mathematical invariants of the Scientific
Behaviour Space.

These invariants guarantee that every Behaviour Space generated by
Jazz Groove Analyzer is scientifically reproducible.

---

# Invariant 1

## Cardinality Preservation

The number of Behaviour Points shall always equal the number of
Geometric Points from which they are constructed.

No Behaviour Point may be created or removed during the transformation.

---

# Invariant 2

## Temporal Ordering

Behaviour Points preserve the chronological ordering of the original
projection.

No reordering is permitted.

---

# Invariant 3

## Deterministic Projection

Given the same Scientific Geometric Plane, the generated Behaviour
Space shall always be identical.

---

# Invariant 4

## Immutability

Scientific Behaviour Space is immutable.

No runtime component may modify its content after construction.

---

# Invariant 5

## Scientific Traceability

Every Behaviour Point shall remain traceable to the Geometric Point
from which it originates.

---

# Consequences

Future analytical algorithms shall preserve all Behaviour Space
invariants.

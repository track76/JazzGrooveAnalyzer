All following calculations remain identical to probe01: 250 ms complete, onset-centred window with no OTHER annotated onset inside; retain exclusions by reason/subtype. Require both classes in all three splits, valid source/annotation hashes and successful converged fit. Missing class/authority => INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE; invalid computation => INDETERMINATE. Stop on either; do not expand the data or repair the model after seeing outcomes.

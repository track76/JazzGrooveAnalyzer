## Frozen Conclusion

`H-VAL001-RHYTHM-CORRESPONDENCE-02` demonstrates that JGA can identify a
limited subset of accompaniment–Drum temporal-comparison candidates from
Ground-Truth-blind observational temporal structure without BPM, meter,
measures, BeatReference, symbolic timing during inference or an arbitrary
millisecond threshold.

The frozen population contains 13 blind candidates: 12 true positives and one
false positive under post-freeze controlled symbolic scoring. Overall
precision is `0.9230769230769231`, recall is `0.2222222222222222`, F1 is
`0.35820895522388063`, and the frozen classification is `LOW_RECALL`.

This is positive-but-conservative evidence. Hypothesis 02 is a
**conservative candidate-discovery rule**, not a complete correspondence
model. The result establishes neither universal correspondence ability nor
generalization beyond the checksum-bound controlled render.

## Demonstrated and Not Demonstrated

The experiment demonstrates that some Piano–Drums and Double Bass–Drums event
relationships can be selected for temporal comparison using mutual unique
geometry and independently recurrent within-source frame context while the
symbolic relationship authority remains hidden during inference.

It does not demonstrate recovery of every authorized relationship,
generalization to another render or performance, applicability to human audio,
swing, other tempi, other arrangements or production fitness. It establishes
no beat, subdivision, musical equivalence, synchronization intent, groove,
swing or performance-quality claim.

The 42 false negatives and one false positive remain frozen validation
evidence. They are not inspected here to design or tune a successor rule. No
Hypothesis 03 is created.

## Current Authority

## Minimum Independent Validation

Before production promotion may be considered, execute exactly one separately
preregistered out-of-sample controlled validation dataset that was not used in
hypothesis construction, the Hypothesis 01 failure-mode audit, Calibration
Zero correspondence development or Hypothesis 02 evaluation.

The minimum dataset shall contain:

- a new checksum-bound authoritative symbolic source;
- independently rendered, checksum-bound Drums, Double Bass and Piano stems;
- explicit sample-zero, sample-rate, frame/hop, rendering, detector,
  PulseCandidate, EME, AD-038 and AD-040 provenance;
- explicit source/asset analytical-role assignments; and
- sufficient authoritative symbolic event identities and relationships for
  post-freeze scoring, kept inaccessible during blind inference.

A controlled swing/walking-bass texture is scientifically useful and preferred
for this next validation because it samples an accompaniment organization
closer to the intended later JGA research population and materially tests
out-of-sample source-density and recurrence behavior. This preference does not
predict improved precision, recall or candidate count and does not authorize
musical inference, BPM or meter use.

## Unchanged-Rule and Ground Truth Firewall

The validation shall apply the complete frozen
`H-VAL001-RHYTHM-CORRESPONDENCE-02` rule unchanged. It may not add, remove or
retune a condition, threshold, tolerance or source-specific exception.

Execution order is mandatory:

1. bind the new controlled assets and observational configuration;
2. establish applicable Calibration Zero authority;
3. construct AD-037 EME, AD-038 geometry and the AD-040 profile without
   symbolic relationship access;
4. execute Hypothesis 02 unchanged;
5. freeze complete candidates, unresolved evidence, fingerprints and
   deterministic replay;
6. only then reveal checksum-bound symbolic authority for scoring; and
7. preserve contributor-specific and overall precision, recall, F1,
   ambiguity, limitations and comparison with the original result without
   retuning.

Ground Truth may score only. It may not create, delete, move, rematch or select
observations or candidates, nor influence rendering, detection, role
assignment, recurrence or nearest-neighbour evidence.

## Calibration Zero Requirement

The new render requires its own provenance-bound Calibration Zero
applicability evidence before correspondence scoring can support promotion.
At minimum this must characterize the combined rendering/measurement behavior
for Drums, Piano and Double Bass and the applicable Piano–Drums and Double
Bass–Drums pairwise context. It must preserve raw observations, uncertainty
and source-specific limitations separately. No correction is implied or
authorized.

Promotion may only be considered after all of the following are frozen and PI

No existing authority defines a numerical precision threshold. None is
introduced here. Recall remains descriptive and the rule remains conservative;
coverage must not be optimized during independent validation.

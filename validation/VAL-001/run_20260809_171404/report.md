## Scientific control

**Observed Fact:** Condition A is byte-identical to the authoritative VAL-001
MusicXML. Condition B was generated by the declared odd-retain/even-remove
symbolic rule. The inventory contains 197 eligible events: 99 retained and 98
removed.

**Observed Fact:** A and B preserve the same symbolic meter, tempo,
instrumentation, retained-event timing, measure identities, and total symbolic
extent. All four WAV assets are stereo 24-bit PCM at 44.1 kHz with 1,983,488
samples per channel.

**Declared Experimental Procedure:** MusicXML score time zero corresponds to
WAV sample zero. No event-correspondence tolerance was introduced.

**Scientific Conclusion:** Under this dataset and the existing discovery

durations. This establishes descriptive invariance evidence only. It does not
identify a beat, tempo, meter, hierarchy, or preferred Candidate Period.

## Reproducibility

Deterministic replay of each canonical WAV produced identical scientific
Candidate Period content fingerprints. Independently supplied repeated WAV
renders produced identical Candidate Period duration/count populations for
their corresponding condition.

The repeated WAV renders are not sample-identical. Condition A differs in
971,111 of 3,966,976 scalar samples; Condition B differs in 261,918. Format,
sample count, first nonzero frame, last nonzero frame, and blind Candidate
Period duration/count populations agree. No tolerance or equivalence threshold
is inferred from these observations.

timestamps. Only authorized reference coordinates, EME and necessary provenance
are allowed inputs. No relationship labels enter candidate generation.

Interior cell edges are exact midpoints between consecutive reference timestamps;
outer edges are the declared scope endpoints. Compare exact authoritative numeric
coordinates without rounding or epsilon. An EME exactly on an interior midpoint
is retained once in a boundary registry, linked to both adjacent cells, and makes

the adjacent cell and is not promoted. Scope-external observations are retained

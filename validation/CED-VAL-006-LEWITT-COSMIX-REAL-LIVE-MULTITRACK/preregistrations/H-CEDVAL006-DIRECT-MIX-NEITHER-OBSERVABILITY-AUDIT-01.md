# Direct-Mix Observability Audit Preregistration

This protocol freezes a read-only spectro-temporal comparison at the original
Bass event coordinates in the controlled mix. It neither runs nor modifies a
detector. Its event-level criterion denotes location-associated mixed-audio
structure only; it does not establish Bass identity, physical onset, or a
production-observer rule.

All parameters, population comparisons, Drum-confusion controls, selection
rules, and decision gates are frozen in the adjacent JSON before population
descriptors are computed.

## Status vocabulary

`SUPPORTED` means the frozen evidence supports the bounded proposition stated
in this map. `PARTIAL` means only part of the frozen gate or proposition was
supported. `NOT_SUPPORTED` means the frozen test did not support its stated
proposition. `INDETERMINATE` means the authority or frozen evidence does not
permit the proposition to be decided. `DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY` means the record
characterizes a population or mechanism but authorizes no prospective rule.
`TECHNICALLY_INFEASIBLE` is reserved for a test that could not technically be
executed. `CLOSED` means the evidence line is complete and should not be
extended by reinterpretation. These are synthesis labels; quoted frozen result
names remain unchanged.

## Cross-family constraints

1. The preserved/missed partition is a correspondence between JGA observation
   populations, not physical-onset Ground Truth.
2. A coordinate labelled `NEGATIVE` is a frozen non-event coordinate, not
   verified Bass absence.
3. Retrospective oracle unions and closest-to-authority selections are
   explanatory quantities only.
4. Evidence that is measurable at a missed coordinate cannot be assigned to
   Double Bass without independent source authority.
5. Correlated harmonic or spectral-envelope measurements do not constitute
   independent mechanisms merely because they have separate names.

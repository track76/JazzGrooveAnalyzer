## Main finding

Preserved events consistently have greater overall harmonic score, stronger
upper-partial evidence, greater upper-partial support relative to the
fundamental, and greater dispersion among partial prominences. These are
continuous associations with preservation status, not a validated observation
or recovery rule. Their magnitudes vary materially, with the largest effects in
CED-VAL-006 and small effects in CED-VAL-005 and CED-VAL-009.

Energy, transient flux, emergence, persistence, fundamental prominence,
harmonic-energy ratio, contrast and F0-crowding measurements do not have a
consistent material-independent effect under the frozen classification.

## Limitations

The analysis is retrospective at frozen Ground-Truth event coordinates. It
does not establish causation, source identity, physical onset, recovery
specificity, or operation without event coordinates. It did not fit a
classifier, combine features, select a threshold or modify JGA. The four
consistent measurements are correlated descriptions of harmonic partial
structure and must not be treated as four independent mechanisms.

## Single recommended next question

On a prospectively selected independent real multitrack, does an event-blind
continuous representation of upper-partial harmonic structure preserve the
same directional association with independently observed Bass events while
retaining explicit ambiguity and without using a binary threshold?

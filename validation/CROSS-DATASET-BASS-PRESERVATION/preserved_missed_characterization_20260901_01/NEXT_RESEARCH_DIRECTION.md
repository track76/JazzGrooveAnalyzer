# Next research direction — record only

The next experiment must use a new, untouched independent real multitrack to
test whether an event-blind continuous representation of upper-partial
harmonic structure retains a directional association with independently
observed Bass events.

The representation must preserve these four continuous dimensions separately:

- maximum harmonic score;
- missing-fundamental balance;
- upper-partial evidence;
- partial-prominence dispersion.

The experiment must not combine them into a fitted scalar score, introduce
thresholds, optimize weights, or use CED-VAL-005, CED-VAL-006, or CED-VAL-009
outcomes for parameter fitting. Ground Truth may be opened only after the
event-blind evidence authority has been frozen and committed.

This document records future research direction only. It does not authorize or
execute an experiment and does not modify JGA behavior.

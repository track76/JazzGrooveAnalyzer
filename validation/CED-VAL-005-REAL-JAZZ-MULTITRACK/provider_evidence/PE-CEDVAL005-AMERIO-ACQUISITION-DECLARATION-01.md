# PE-CEDVAL005-AMERIO-ACQUISITION-DECLARATION-01

Status: **PRESERVED PROVIDER DOCUMENTARY EVIDENCE — BOUNDED AUTHORITY**

## Provenance

On 2026-08-31 the Principal Investigator supplied the repository with a
substantive transcription of a direct written response from Stefano Amerio,
recording engineer / Artesuono, concerning the Cambridge Music Technology
multitrack of Maurizio Pagnutti Sextet's “All The Gin Is Gone.” The original
correspondence and its message headers were not supplied to this repository;
this record therefore preserves the PI-supplied transcription without
claiming archival custody of the original message. The adjacent JSON is the
machine-readable authority.

The supplied exact clarification is:

> “le registrazioni non hanno nulla di alterato a livello di timing”

The remaining statements are preserved as the PI's faithful substantive
transcription, not as verbatim quotation.

## Provider statements preserved

- The performance was recorded live/direct, with musicians in separate rooms
  and without overdubs; the distributed material represents that performance.
- The tracks were recorded together into Pro Tools through Digi 192
  interfaces in one recording operation.
- The distributed WAV files preserve the original musical temporal
  relationship. Track beginnings and endings were cleaned before portal
  export.
- No quantization or warp was applied. Perhaps one or two very small alignment
  operations occurred; they were described as minimal.
- `09_Overheads.wav` and `11_BassDI.wav` belong to the same direct
  performance.
- BassDI is the direct Bass signal and was phase-aligned with the Bass
  microphone to avoid frequency cancellation.

## Bounded authority consequence

This evidence establishes attributable provider authority for the selected
tracks belonging to one simultaneous, no-overdub performance and one stated
Pro Tools / Digi 192 recording operation. It strengthens preservation of the
musical-performance temporal relationship and establishes a provider
declaration of no quantization and no warp.

It does not establish a single hardware clock configuration: the number and
clock topology of the Digi 192 interfaces were not supplied. It does not
establish an exact sample-zero mapping to acquisition time: beginnings and
endings were cleaned before export and no offsets were supplied. It does not
establish absence of all sample-level transformations: possible minimal
alignments are disclosed, and BassDI phase alignment is explicit. It does not
establish physical-source onset Ground Truth, detector accuracy, event
correspondence, calibration applicability or sample-accurate human
microtiming Ground Truth.

Accordingly the prospective acquisition gate remains
`ACQUISITION_AUTHORITY_PARTIAL`; no frozen CED-VAL-005 experiment or
historical result is reinterpreted or overwritten.

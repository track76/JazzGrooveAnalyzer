# CED-VAL-005 next research direction — 2026-09-01

Status: **PI-authorized future research direction only; not implemented and not an experiment.**

## Maximum Defensible Observation Principle

JGA should attempt to recover the maximum amount of signal-grounded Double Bass evidence that can be scientifically defended. It must not invent, interpolate, statistically reconstruct, or musically infer events merely to complete the performance.

Analysis must be performed only where sufficient observational evidence exists. Where sufficient evidence does not exist, the system must preserve the absence or ambiguity explicitly rather than manufacture an observation.

Future representation may therefore investigate conceptual states such as `ANALYZABLE`, `NOT_ANALYZABLE`, and `INDETERMINATE`. These are research-direction terms only. They are not a permanent schema or ontology and are not authorized for implementation without separate evidence and PI approval.

## Recovery research principle

The next research phase may investigate recovery of additional Bass observations. Its objective is not to reach a predetermined recall percentage. Its objective is to recover as much additional Bass evidence as the signal itself supports without introducing unsupported observations.

Ground Truth may be used for scientific validation of a proposed method, but must not be information available to a production detector. Any future method must be signal-grounded, event-blind in production, independently validated, provenance-preserving, uncertainty-aware, and capable of declining analysis where evidence is insufficient.

The prior failed or insufficient blind pitched-Bass experiments remain valid evidence and must not be silently retuned or overwritten. A next recovery study requires a new preregistration and an evidence-based method justification before implementation.

## Interpretation boundary

`H-CEDVAL005-RHYTHM-SECTION-TIMING-SUFFICIENCY-01` established only that the currently incomplete CED-VAL-005 separated-audio observation population cannot be treated as a sufficient representation of the complete original-source timing profile under its frozen gates. It does not establish that every missing Bass event must be reconstructed, that any fixed recall is required, that missing observations may be inferred, or that existing observable events are scientifically unusable. Existing observations remain valid within their authorized evidential scope.

No recovery experiment, detector optimization, Ground-Truth-informed production rule, state implementation, AD-037/AD-038/AD-040 change, RhythmSectionTimingProfile change, or other JGA behavior change is authorized by this record.
